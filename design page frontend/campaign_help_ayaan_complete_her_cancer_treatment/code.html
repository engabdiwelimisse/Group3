Help Ayaan Complete Her Cancer Treatment

Organized by Ibrahim Hersi

Created 3 days ago

My name is Ibrahim, and I am starting this campaign for my sister, Ayaan. Recently, Ayaan was diagnosed with Stage II breast cancer. As a vibrant 32-year-old teacher and mother of two, this news has devastated our family, but we are determined to fight this together.

The Treatment Plan

Ayaan's medical team in Hargeisa has outlined a comprehensive treatment plan that includes surgery followed by a series of chemotherapy sessions. While the hospital provides excellent care, the costs of the specific chemotherapy drugs and the surgical procedure are beyond what our family can afford on our own.

How Your Contribution Helps

The total cost for the complete treatment cycle is estimated at $20,000. We have exhausted our savings and received incredible support from our immediate community, raising $15,000 so far. We are now reaching out for help to bridge the remaining $5,000 gap.

  • Surgery: Scheduled for next month to remove the tumor.
  • Chemotherapy: 6 cycles over the next 6 months.
  • Medication & Aftercare: Essential support for her recovery process.

Every contribution, no matter the size, brings Ayaan one step closer to recovery and returning to her classroom and her children. Thank you for your generosity, prayers, and for sharing her story.

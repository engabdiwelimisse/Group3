How Kaalmo Works

Connecting organizers with donors through a transparent, verified, and community-driven process.

campaign

For Organizers

Start your campaign and reach the community in three simple steps.

1

Create

Tell your story clearly. Set your funding goal and detail exactly how the funds will be utilized.

2

Verify

Our team reviews your campaign. Submit necessary documents to earn the Kaalmo Verification Badge.

3

Share

Once approved, share your campaign link with your network and the wider Kaalmo community.

volunteer_activism

For Donors

Support verified causes with confidence and track the impact of your contributions.

1

Discover

Browse campaigns by category or location to find causes that resonate with your values.

2

Verify

Look for the Verification Badge. Review the organizer's details and the campaign's breakdown of expenses.

3

Donate

Contribute securely. Receive transparent updates on how funds are utilized by the organizer.

Ready to make an impact?

Whether you are organizing a community effort or looking to support one, Kaalmo provides the secure platform you need.

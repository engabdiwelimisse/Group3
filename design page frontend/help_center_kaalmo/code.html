Frequently Asked Questions

How long does verification take? expand_more
Standard identity verification typically takes 1-2 business days. If additional documentation is required, our Trust & Safety team will reach out directly.
What are the platform fees? expand_more
Kaalmo charges a small, transparent fee to maintain the platform's security and infrastructure. Payment processors also apply a standard transaction fee. View our detailed fee schedule.
Can I donate anonymously? expand_more
Yes. When making a donation, you can choose to hide your name and donation amount from the public campaign page. Only the campaign organizer will see your details for thank-you purposes.
